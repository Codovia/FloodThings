## Task B — automatic location, local weather and Home

The regression was a frontend/App/backend all-purpose 100 m cutoff plus a reverse provider requiring unconfigured reviewed MapTiler credentials. Positioning is now purpose-specific: browser uncertainty up to 10 km supports explicitly approximate point weather; up to 5 km permits an approximate provider locality label. These are application policies, not certified positioning accuracy. Reviewed MapTiler naming still requires its locality polygon to cover the full uncertainty circle. River-specific context retains its separate 100 m and reviewed geometry gates. Network estimates never establish district/river identity.

An automatic browser attempt runs on a fresh application load, bounded to 10 seconds. Accepted coordinates immediately load Open-Meteo. Naming discovers configured provider without sending coordinates; reviewed MapTiler is preferred. Otherwise fresh consented coordinates go directly from the browser to BigDataCloud. Coarse fixes, denial or timeout try a separate coordinate-free network locality estimate, never relabel the coarse fix as precise GPS. Its uncertainty is unknown and displayed. Naming has a separate 10-second bound, no background retries or watchPosition. Manual selection cancels pending work and late responses cannot replace it. Manual/reviewed points are never submitted to the free client geocoder.

Provider documentation was checked on 13 October: [endpoint contract](https://www.bigdatacloud.com/free-api/free-reverse-geocode-to-city-api), [fair-use policy](https://www.bigdatacloud.com/docs/article/fair-use-policy-for-free-client-side-reverse-geocoding-api), [official client](https://github.com/bigdatacloudapi/js-reverse-geocode-client). Direct device calls and fresh consented location are required; no server proxy, stored-coordinate queries or bulk use. The UI explains coordinate/network pairing and attribution. Official provider blog and client documentation disagree on lookupSource spellings; the adapter accepts only the documented coordinate and IP variants and verifies coordinate echoes. No public Nominatim integration was added.
